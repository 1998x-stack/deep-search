# Contradiction and adversarial research

Use a pairwise contradiction table with: claim A, claim B, source IDs, exact scope/time, strength of each source, and what new evidence would resolve the conflict.

Not all disagreements are contradictions. First harmonize jurisdiction, statistical definition, measurement window, version, and whether statements are predictions versus observations.

Search for:
- original corrections and retractions;
- independent reproductions and failed reproductions;
- alternative explanations and confounders;
- legal holdings versus allegations;
- policy intent versus measured outcomes;
- changed definitions over time.

Do not give false balance: summarize the strongest substantiated account, then clearly indicate minority or poorly supported positions. Never average contradictory numbers when measuring different quantities.

A useful Critic output specifies what would falsify the leading hypothesis, which evidence could do so, and whether it was found.
