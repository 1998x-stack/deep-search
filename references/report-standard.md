# Research report contract

## Recommended report

1. Executive answer: what is known, not known, and likely.
2. Question, scope, date and methodology.
3. Core findings linked to source IDs `[S001]`.
4. Evidence chronology and data/methodology.
5. Contradiction matrix and strongest counterevidence.
6. Inferences clearly distinct from supported facts.
7. Limitations, unavailable documents and residual uncertainty.
8. Sources: unique source ID, full URL, publisher and date.
9. Reproducibility: search log, plan and ledger paths where appropriate.

Make material claims granular enough to audit. Link to the original sources, not just search results. Include citation markers in the narrative, not only in bibliography entries. Avoid lengthy unlicensed quotations.

## Automated output caveat

The `deep-search report` command emits a **mechanical evidence inventory** from an already prepared plan/ledger; it does not independently produce a research narrative, weigh competing theories or validate truth. The `audit` command checks IDs and quote presence, not factual entailment, page accessibility, or relevance.

Reports cannot pass merely by citing many unrelated sources. Human or agent reviewers must verify whether each quoted source actually supports the associated claim.
